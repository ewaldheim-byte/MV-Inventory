MV Inventory Privacy Policy

MV Inventory is a private inventory-management application used to organize, verify, and manage physical inventory and eBay listings.

Information Accessed

With authorization from the eBay account owner, MV Inventory may access eBay listing information including listing titles, item identifiers, available quantities, listing status, item condition, and listing images.

Information Stored

MV Inventory may retain listing information and reference images for inventory identification, verification, fulfillment, historical tracking, and catalog generation.

Use of Information

Information obtained from eBay is used only for inventory-management functions related to MV Inventory. Information is not sold to third parties.

Authentication and Security

Access to eBay account information is authorized through eBay's OAuth authorization system. Sensitive application credentials are not intentionally exposed through the MV Inventory mobile application.

Marketplace Account Deletion

MV Inventory supports eBay Marketplace Account Deletion notifications. Applicable eBay-derived user data will be processed or deleted as required when a valid marketplace account deletion notification is received.

Data Retention

Inventory records and reference images may be retained for inventory, transaction-history, fulfillment, and catalog purposes unless deletion is required by applicable policy or law.

Contact

Questions concerning this privacy policy may be directed to the MV Inventory application owner.
